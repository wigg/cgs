About Svendsen Sounds

Svendsen Sounds gives an electric car the live sound of an engine. Open it in the car's browser (or on a phone connected to the car's speakers), press Start engine, allow location access and drive. The engine follows your real speed.

How it works

Every engine sound is synthesized live in your browser. There are no recordings or loops: a small model of the engine and drivetrain turns your speed and acceleration into revs, load, gear changes, exhaust pulses and crackle, and plays them through the Web Audio API.

Free

Svendsen Sounds is free. All cars and unlimited engine time, no account and no subscription.

Not affiliated

Svendsen Sounds is not affiliated with Tesla or with any car maker. Car names are used only to describe the character of the sound.
