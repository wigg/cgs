Privacy

Short version: Svendsen Sounds does not collect anything about you.

Location

The browser's location (GPS) is used only to measure your speed, and only while the engine is running. It is processed in your browser and is never sent to a server.

What is stored on your device

Your settings (chosen car, volume, units and so on) are saved in your browser's local storage so they are still there next time. They stay on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

No tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies, and it does not collect any personal data. The typeface is hosted on the site itself, so no request goes to a third party.

Hosting

Like any website, whoever hosts the files may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address and time of a request). Svendsen Sounds itself does not use them.
