Svendsen Sounds

Phone sensor

Your phone feels every push of the throttle and brake at once. It sends that to the car, so the engine sound reacts faster than GPS alone.

Open this page by scanning the QR code in the car (Svendsen Sounds, More settings, Phone sensor), or type the code shown there.

Ready

stillstrong push

  1. Put the phone in a holder or on a non-slip mat. Any direction works.
  2. Press Start and keep this screen open. The screen stays on while it runs.
  3. Drive. The car works out which way is forward after a few accelerations.

Nothing is recorded. Only acceleration numbers are sent, and only to your own car through a private link.
